Who We Are

Shekhawati International Academy (SIA) is located on Sikar Road, Losal, in Sikar district, Rajasthan. Established in 2008, the school aims to develop knowledgeable, thoughtful and responsible students.

The school emphasizes value-based education by combining academic learning with practical understanding and respect for cultural values. Its goal is to encourage students to learn with curiosity, confidence and a desire to improve.

Our Vision

To help students grow into confident, independent and responsible individuals who can contribute positively to society.

Our Educational Goals

  • Encourage curiosity and independent learning.
  • Develop academic knowledge and practical skills.
  • Promote honesty, respect and compassion.
  • Support intellectual, social and physical growth.
  • Encourage teamwork and healthy competition.
