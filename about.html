About Shekhawati International Academy

Founded in 2008, SIA is a CBSE-affiliated school in Losal, Sikar, with classes from Nursery to XII.

Education sets one free

Curiosity, confidence, and community.

SIA's official about page describes an approach that balances practical and theoretical teaching and encourages students to seek knowledge with curiosity. The school says its education is rooted in cultural heritage and value-based learning.

Its motto, “Knowledge Strength Compassion,” reflects the school's stated commitment to academic excellence, extracurricular activities, and students' overall development.

SIA is affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education for the All India Secondary and Senior Secondary Examinations.
